# Skill Spotter

Use this after repeated review comments or session-mining findings.

## Steps

1. Count repeated corrections and identify the underlying rule.
2. Decide whether the rule is durable, reusable, and teachable.
3. Choose the smallest surface: AGENTS rule, skill, CI rule, or checklist.
4. Draft the trigger, steps, and output contract if a skill is warranted.

## Output

Say "skill", "AGENTS rule", "CI rule", or "no durable artifact yet", with one
paragraph of reasoning.
